Homemade Cinnamon Rolls | Soft, Gooey & Better Than Cinnabon

These Homemade Cinnamon Rolls are soft, pillowy, and loaded with cinnamon sugar filling topped with the most decadent cream cheese frosting. Better than Cinnabon and worth every minute of baking!

Ingredients

  • 3 cups flour
  • one packet yeast
  • half cup warm milk
  • quarter cup sugar
  • quarter cup butter
  • 1 egg
  • salt
  • For the filling: half cup brown sugar
  • 2 tablespoons cinnamon
  • quarter cup softened butter
  • For frosting: cream cheese
  • powdered sugar
  • vanilla
  • butter

Instructions

These Homemade Cinnamon Rolls are impossibly soft, oozing with cinnamon sugar, and topped with the creamiest frosting. Better than anything from a bakery and your kitchen will smell absolutely amazing.

Step 1

You'll need: 3 cups flour, one packet yeast, half cup warm milk, quarter cup sugar, quarter cup butter, 1 egg, and salt. For the filling: half cup brown sugar, 2 tablespoons cinnamon, quarter cup softened butter. For frosting: cream cheese, powdered sugar, vanilla, and butter.

Step 2

Dissolve the yeast in warm milk with a pinch of sugar. Let it sit for 5 minutes until it's bubbly and alive. Mix it into the flour with sugar, melted butter, egg, and salt. Knead for 8 minutes until smooth and elastic.

Step 3

Let the dough rise in a warm spot for 1 hour until doubled in size. Then roll it out into a large rectangle about a quarter inch thick. Spread softened butter all over, then generously sprinkle with brown sugar and cinnamon.

Step 4

Roll the dough tightly into a log starting from the long side. Use dental floss or a sharp knife to slice into 12 equal rolls. Place them in a buttered baking dish with a little space between each one.

Step 5

Let the rolls rise for another 30 minutes until puffy, then bake at 375 degrees for 22 to 25 minutes until golden brown. They should be soft and slightly underdone in the center.

While they're still warm, spread the cream cheese frosting generously over the top. Watch it melt into every crevice. These homemade cinnamon rolls are pure heaven. Soft, gooey, and absolutely irresistible.
