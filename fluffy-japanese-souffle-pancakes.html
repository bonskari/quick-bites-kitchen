Instructions

This Japanese souffle pancake is so fluffy, it’s like eating clouds! Perfect for breakfast or dessert, no takeout needed.

Step 1

You’ll need flour, sugar, eggs, milk, baking powder, and a touch of vanilla for that perfect flavor balance.

Step 2

Whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder until smooth. Add eggs and milk gradually for a silky batter.

Step 3

Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat. Pour 1/4 cup batter for perfect round pancakes.

Step 4

Let cook 1-2 minutes until edges set. Gently flip with spatula for even cooking and fluffy texture.

Step 5

Top with kinako powder for a nutty flavor or matcha for a vibrant green twist. Drizzle with sweetened condensed milk.

Step 6

Stack warm pancakes with a touch of whipped cream and fresh berries for a breakfast masterpiece.
