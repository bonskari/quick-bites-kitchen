Easy Homemade Ramen | Rich Broth in 20 Minutes

Skip the instant packets! This Easy Homemade Ramen has a rich, savory broth, tender noodles, a jammy egg, and all the toppings — and it's ready in just 20 minutes.

Comfort Food Quick Dinners
Prep: 5 min Cook: 15 min Servings: 2 Calories: 450

Ingredients

  • 2 packs ramen noodles (discard seasoning packets)
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon miso paste
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • 2 soft-boiled eggs
  • 1 cup baby spinach or bok choy
  • 4 slices chashu pork or chicken
  • Green onions, nori, sesame seeds for garnish
  • Chili oil (optional)

Instructions

Real ramen flavor doesn't need hours of simmering. This shortcut recipe delivers a rich, complex broth in just 20 minutes. It's miles ahead of any instant ramen!

Step 1

Bring chicken broth to a simmer. Whisk in soy sauce, miso paste, and sesame oil. Let it simmer for 5 minutes to develop flavor.

Step 2

Cook ramen noodles according to package directions (usually 3 minutes). Drain and divide between two large bowls.

Step 3

For the jammy egg: boil eggs for exactly 6.5 minutes, then immediately transfer to an ice bath. Peel and halve when cool.

Step 4

Add spinach or bok choy to the simmering broth for 1 minute until wilted. Ladle the hot broth over the noodles.

Step 5

Top each bowl with a halved jammy egg, sliced pork or chicken, green onions, nori, sesame seeds, and a drizzle of chili oil!

The miso is the secret ingredient that gives this quick broth incredible depth. Customize with your favorite toppings — corn, mushrooms, bean sprouts, or whatever you love!
