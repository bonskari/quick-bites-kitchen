Chocolate Lava Cake | Molten Center in 15 Minutes

This Chocolate Lava Cake has a perfectly gooey, molten center surrounded by a delicate cake exterior. Only 5 ingredients and 15 minutes — the most impressive dessert you can make!

Baking & Sweets
Prep: 5 min Cook: 12 min Servings: 2 Calories: 420

Ingredients

  • 4 oz dark chocolate (70%+)
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • Pinch of salt
  • Powdered sugar and vanilla ice cream for serving

Instructions

Restaurant-quality chocolate lava cake at home? Yes! Only 5 main ingredients and 15 minutes. The secret is all in the timing.

Step 1

Preheat oven to 425°F (220°C). Generously butter two ramekins and dust with cocoa powder. This ensures the cakes release perfectly.

Step 2

Melt dark chocolate and butter together in a microwave (30-second intervals, stirring) or double boiler until smooth and glossy.

Step 3

Whisk eggs, sugar, and salt until slightly frothy. Pour the melted chocolate into the egg mixture and stir to combine. Fold in the flour gently.

Step 4

Divide batter between the prepared ramekins. Bake for exactly 12 minutes — the edges should be set but the center should jiggle slightly.

Step 5

Let cool for 1 minute, then run a knife around the edge and invert onto a plate. Dust with powdered sugar and serve immediately with vanilla ice cream!

The 12-minute bake time is crucial — even 1 minute too long and the center won't be molten! This is the ultimate date night dessert. Your guest will be speechless.
