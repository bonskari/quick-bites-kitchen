Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta | 20-Minute One-Pan Dinner

This Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta is a flavor explosion! Tender chicken, perfectly al dente penne, bell peppers, and a rich, spicy cajun cream sauce — all in one pan in just 20 minutes.

cajun chicken pasta creamy pasta one pan dinner quick dinner cajun recipe easy pasta
Quick Dinners
Prep: 5 min Cook: 15 min Servings: 4 Calories: 520

Ingredients

  • 2 large chicken breasts, sliced
  • 8 oz penne pasta
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1/2 cup chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons cajun seasoning
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese, grated
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • Fresh parsley for garnish
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

If you love bold, spicy flavors with creamy comfort, this Cajun Chicken Pasta will blow your mind. It's like a restaurant dish but done in 20 minutes at home!

Step 1

Cook the penne pasta according to package directions until al dente. Reserve 1/2 cup pasta water before draining.

Step 2

Season chicken slices generously with cajun seasoning. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Sear chicken until golden and cooked through, about 5-6 minutes. Remove and set aside.

Step 3

In the same skillet, sauté bell peppers and garlic for 2 minutes until softened. Pour in heavy cream and chicken broth, stirring to combine.

Step 4

Add parmesan cheese and stir until the sauce is smooth and creamy. Toss in the cooked pasta and chicken. Add reserved pasta water if needed to thin the sauce.

Step 5

Garnish with fresh parsley and extra parmesan. Serve immediately while hot and creamy!

This Creamy Cajun Chicken Pasta is an instant crowd-pleaser. Adjust the cajun seasoning to your spice preference. Save this recipe for your next weeknight dinner!
