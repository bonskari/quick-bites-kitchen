Instructions

Hey bakers! Today we’re making fluffy cinnamon sugar churros that are crispy on the outside and gooey inside. Let’s get those sweet cravings satisfied!

Step 1

Grab your ingredients: flour, eggs, milk, butter, cinnamon, sugar, and frying oil. These churros will be worth every step!

Step 2

Mix flour, eggs, milk, and melted butter until smooth. Add a pinch of salt for flavor and let the dough rest for 10 minutes.

Step 3

Shape the dough into long ropes, dip in cinnamon sugar, and fry in hot oil until golden. Watch those bubbles form!

Step 4

Fry churros in batches until crispy and airy. Use a slotted spoon to drain excess oil for that perfect crunch!

Step 5

Toss fried churros in extra cinnamon sugar while warm. These will be the best you’ve ever had—trust me!

Step 6

Serve with a scoop of vanilla ice cream or a drizzle of caramel. These churros are pure happiness in every bite!
