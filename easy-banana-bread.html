Easy Banana Bread | Moist, Sweet & Perfect Every Time

The best Easy Banana Bread recipe you'll ever make. Incredibly moist, naturally sweet from ripe bananas, with a golden crust. One bowl, simple ingredients, and ready in under an hour. A timeless classic!

Ingredients

  • 3 very ripe bananas
  • one-third cup melted butter
  • three-quarters cup sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • a pinch of salt
  • 1 and a half cups all-purpose flour

Instructions

This Easy Banana Bread is moist, sweet, and absolutely foolproof. If you've got overripe bananas on your counter, you're about to make the best thing that ever came out of your oven.

Step 1

You'll need: 3 very ripe bananas, one-third cup melted butter, three-quarters cup sugar, 1 egg, 1 teaspoon vanilla extract, 1 teaspoon baking soda, a pinch of salt, and 1 and a half cups all-purpose flour.

Step 2

Preheat your oven to 350 degrees Fahrenheit. Mash the ripe bananas in a large bowl with a fork until smooth. The riper the bananas, the sweeter and more flavorful your bread will be.

Step 3

Stir the melted butter into the mashed bananas. Mix in the sugar, beaten egg, and vanilla extract. Then add baking soda and salt, and gently fold in the flour until just combined. Don't overmix!

Step 4

Pour the batter into a greased 9x5 inch loaf pan. For an extra touch, slice a banana lengthwise and place it on top. Bake for 55 to 60 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

Step 5

Let the bread cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack. The top should be golden brown and cracked, and the inside incredibly moist. Slice it thick and enjoy.

Serve warm with a pat of butter melting on top, or enjoy it plain. This banana bread stays moist for days and tastes even better the next day. The simplest recipe with the most incredible results.
