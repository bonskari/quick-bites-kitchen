Crispy Honey Garlic Shrimp | Sweet & Savory 20-Minute Dinner

Crispy Honey Garlic Shrimp with a sticky, sweet glaze that's absolutely addictive. Perfectly crispy shrimp tossed in a homemade honey garlic sauce. Ready in 20 minutes and better than takeout!

Ingredients

  • 1 pound large shrimp peeled and deveined
  • half cup cornstarch
  • 3 tablespoons honey
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • red pepper flakes
  • sesame seeds
  • green onions

Instructions

These Crispy Honey Garlic Shrimp are sweet, savory, sticky, and completely addictive. Golden crispy shrimp coated in the most incredible honey garlic glaze. Way better than takeout!

Step 1

You'll need: 1 pound large shrimp peeled and deveined, half cup cornstarch, 3 tablespoons honey, 2 tablespoons soy sauce, 4 cloves garlic, 1 tablespoon rice vinegar, red pepper flakes, sesame seeds, and green onions.

Step 2

Pat the shrimp dry and toss them in cornstarch until every piece is evenly coated. This is what gives them that irresistible crispy texture that holds up beautifully in the sauce.

Step 3

Heat oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Fry the cornstarch-coated shrimp for 2 minutes per side until golden and crispy. Work in batches to avoid crowding. Set the crispy shrimp aside.

Step 4

In the same pan, add minced garlic and cook for 30 seconds. Then add honey, soy sauce, and rice vinegar. Stir until the sauce bubbles and thickens into a beautiful sticky glaze.

Step 5

Toss the crispy shrimp back into the pan and coat every piece in that incredible honey garlic sauce. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and sliced green onions. The glaze should be thick and coating every shrimp.

Serve immediately over steamed rice or with noodles. These honey garlic shrimp are sweet, savory, crispy, and gone in minutes. Twenty minutes from start to finish for the most incredible dinner.
