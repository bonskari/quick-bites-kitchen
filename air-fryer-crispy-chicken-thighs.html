Air Fryer Crispy Chicken Thighs

Get ultra-crispy, juicy chicken thighs in your air fryer in just 25 minutes! This easy recipe delivers restaurant-quality results with minimal oil. Perfect for busy weeknights.

Ingredients

  • 6 bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried thyme
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • Fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

Ready for the crispiest chicken thighs you've ever made without deep frying? Your air fryer is about to become your best friend! These come out with shatteringly crispy skin and incredibly juicy meat every single time.

1

Pat your chicken thighs completely dry with paper towels. This is the most important step for crispy skin! Remove any excess moisture from both sides.

2

In a small bowl, mix together the garlic powder, smoked paprika, onion powder, dried thyme, salt, and pepper. Drizzle the chicken with olive oil and rub the spice mixture all over, getting under the skin too.

3

Preheat your air fryer to 400°F (200°C) for 3 minutes. Place the chicken thighs skin-side down in the basket in a single layer. Don't overcrowd — cook in batches if needed.

4

Air fry for 10 minutes skin-side down, then flip the thighs over so skin is facing up. Continue cooking for 12-15 minutes until the internal temperature reaches 165°F (74°C) and the skin is deeply golden and crispy.

5

Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving. This allows the juices to redistribute throughout the meat, keeping it incredibly moist and tender.

6

Serve with your favorite sides like roasted vegetables, mashed potatoes, or a crisp green salad. Garnish with fresh parsley for a pop of color.

And that's it — perfectly crispy air fryer chicken thighs with minimal effort! The skin is like crackling and the meat falls right off the bone. Save this recipe and try it tonight!
