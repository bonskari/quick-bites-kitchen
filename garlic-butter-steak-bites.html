Garlic Butter Steak Bites | 15-Minute Restaurant Quality

These Garlic Butter Steak Bites are tender, juicy, and swimming in the most incredible garlic butter sauce. Made with sirloin steak in just 15 minutes. Better than any steakhouse and perfect for a quick dinner!

Ingredients

  • 1.5 pounds sirloin steak cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 6 cloves garlic minced
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • fresh thyme and rosemary
  • salt
  • pepper
  • olive oil

Instructions

These Garlic Butter Steak Bites are absolute perfection. Tender, juicy cubes of steak tossed in a rich garlic butter sauce. Restaurant quality in just 15 minutes flat.

Step 1

You'll need: 1.5 pounds sirloin steak cut into 1-inch cubes, 4 tablespoons butter, 6 cloves garlic minced, 1 tablespoon soy sauce, fresh thyme and rosemary, salt, pepper, and olive oil.

Step 2

Pat the steak cubes completely dry with paper towels. This is the secret to getting a perfect sear. Season generously with salt and pepper on all sides.

Step 3

Heat olive oil in a cast iron skillet over high heat until it's smoking hot. Add the steak bites in a single layer — don't overcrowd the pan. Sear for 2 minutes per side until deeply browned.

Step 4

Reduce heat to medium. Add butter, minced garlic, and fresh herbs to the skillet. As the butter melts and foams, spoon it over the steak bites continuously for 2 minutes. This is restaurant-level basting.

Step 5

Add a splash of soy sauce and toss everything together. The garlic butter sauce should coat every single piece of steak. The aroma is absolutely incredible.

Serve these garlic butter steak bites immediately while they're hot and juicy. Pair with mashed potatoes, rice, or crusty bread to soak up that amazing sauce. Fifteen minutes to steakhouse perfection.
